The plan

What am I making? → What's being chosen, from what? → Kinds or a rule? Split, or start with the most restricting part → Swap test → Build it and multiply → Built anything twice? Divide → Check a tiny case.

Moves

  1. Group of different things, order doesn't matter: n choose k = n!/(k!·(n − k)!).
  2. Groups one after another: choose each from whoever is left, and multiply.
  3. Same-size groups with no names: divide by the number of ways to name them.
  4. Repeated letters or grid paths: choose the spots.
  5. Checkpoint or two independent decisions: multiply. Kinds that can't overlap: add.

Answers

Type a number or an expression: 12 × 11 ÷ 4, 2^6 × 11, 12!/(4!·8!), C(12, 4) and '12 choose 4' all work. Lists can be written “8 and 3”.
